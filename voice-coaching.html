Voice Coaching

Are you interested in learning to sing, or want to improve the singing you already do? This could be for you.

I pursued a career as a singer-songwriter, releasing several albums and performing in venues both small and large, as well as singing in choirs. That experience is the foundation for the lessons I teach today.

Unlike my accent modification services, voice lessons are offered in person only, in the East Bay — I'll come to you, right in your own home.

Curious if voice lessons are a fit? Book a free 15-minute consultation call, no obligation.
