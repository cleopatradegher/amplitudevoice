Singing Lessons

Anyone can learn how to improve their singing voice. All experience levels are welcome, and I would love to work with you.

I work with kids (starting around age 8), teens, and adults. My focus is on popular music singing, including pop, rock, R&B, soul, country, folk, and jazz-influenced pop. I don't teach classical singing or traditional jazz.

I'm a trained singer with years of performing experience, and my background in speech-language pathology gives me a deep understanding of how the voice works, including projecting your voice without straining. You can listen to what my voice sounds like in one of my songs on Bandcamp.

Each lesson blends voice warm-ups, work on the essentials like breath, posture, and range, specific vocal techniques, and time spent singing songs you enjoy. You'll get supportive, specific feedback and a simple thing or two to practice between lessons. Kids' lessons are 30 minutes long; teen and adult lessons are 45 minutes long.

The best first step is a free 15-minute consultation call, where we can talk about what genres and styles of singing you like and what you want to work on.

Pricing

  • Free 15-minute consultation call. We talk about your goals and any concerns regarding your singing voice, as well as what genres of singing you're interested in working on.
  • Kids' lesson (30 minutes): $45
  • Teen and adult lesson (45 minutes): $65

Lessons take place in your home, within about 15 minutes of El Cerrito, usually weekly. Pay as you go, one lesson at a time.
