Singing Lessons

Anyone can learn how to improve their singing voice. All experience levels are welcome, and I would love to work with you.

I work with kids (starting around age 8), teens, and adults. My focus is on popular music singing, including pop, rock, R&B, soul, country, folk, and jazz.

I'm a trained singer with years of performing experience, and my background in speech-language pathology gives me a deep understanding of how the voice works, including how to project your voice without strain and how to manipulate the timbre of your voice. You can listen to what my voice sounds like in one of my songs on Bandcamp.

A typical lesson consists of voice and body warm-ups, targeted vocal exercises, and singing a song or two. We'll work on the student's challenge areas, which can include things like expanding their range, sustaining long notes, reducing breathiness, strengthening chest voice or head voice, developing the mix (the crossover area between chest and head voice), and reducing strain. You'll get supportive, specific feedback throughout the lesson.
