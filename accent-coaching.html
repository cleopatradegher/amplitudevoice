Accent Coaching (of the General American accent)

Do you feel like you often have to repeat yourself to be understood? Or do you feel like others sometimes focus more on how you're saying something than on what you're saying, even though your English is excellent?

Together, we can work to improve your clarity. You'll still sound like you — we will not erase your accent, but we will modify the aspects of it that get in the way of effective communication.

More than anything, this is about confidence. When you're not worried about being understood, you can focus on what you're actually saying — in a meeting, an interview, or just everyday conversation.

Curious if one-on-one accent coaching sessions are for you? Book a free 15-minute consultation call, no obligation.
