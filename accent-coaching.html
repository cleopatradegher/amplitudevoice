Accent Coaching

Accent coaching helps you speak English more clearly and confidently, so people focus on what you're saying, not how you're saying it.

Do you often have to repeat yourself to be understood? Do you feel people notice how you speak more than what you say, even though your English is excellent?

Together we'll work on your clarity. You'll still sound like you. We won't erase your accent; we'll soften the parts of it that get in the way of being understood.

Most of all, this is about confidence. When you're not worried about being understood, you can focus on what you actually want to say, in a meeting, an interview, or everyday conversation.

I specialize in the General American accent. If you're hoping to work toward a different accent, let me know on our consultation call and we can talk about it.

If you're interested to learn more about how accent coaching can help you with your communication, schedule a free consultation.
