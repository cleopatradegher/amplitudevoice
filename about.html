About

I love working with people one-on-one. The part I find most rewarding is helping someone get clear on what they're working toward, and then being in their corner while they get there — at their own pace, on their own goals.

I studied linguistics before going on to a master's in speech pathology. I'm also a singer, and pursued a music career of my own for years — so I know first-hand what it takes to train a voice: the practice, the patience, and the nerve it takes to be heard.
