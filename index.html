About

Learning to use your voice differently is a skill, not a mystery. It takes practice and good feedback, and you don't have to work it out on your own.

We start with zeroing in on your goals, then build toward them in steps, at a pace that suits your life. The point isn't to make you sound like someone else. It's to give you more range, more control, and more confidence when you speak (or sing), to sound like you.

Working one-on-one is the part I like best. My background is in linguistics and speech-language pathology, where I honed my skills in noticing the small things that make a big difference in a person's communication. I'm also a singer — I spent years pursuing a music career — so I know what consistent practice feels like, and the glorious feeling when you realize a skill you were struggling with has become second nature.

So reach out to schedule a free consultation. I would love to be a part of your learning journey!
