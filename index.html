About

Using your voice differently is a skill you can learn. It takes practice and good feedback, and you don't have to figure it out on your own.

I'm Cleopatra Degher, the coach at Amplitude Accent and Voice. I listen to my clients' goals and priorities, then we build toward them step by step. The goal isn't to make you sound like someone else. It's to give you more confidence and control when you speak or sing.

I come to this work as a native speaker of both English and Swedish. My background is in linguistics (BA) and speech-language pathology (MA), where I learned to notice the small things that make a big difference in how someone communicates. I'm also a singer who spent years performing, so I know what steady practice feels like, and I've experienced struggling with something new that then became second nature.

Ready to start? Schedule a free consultation. I'd love to be part of your learning journey.
