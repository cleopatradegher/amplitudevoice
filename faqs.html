FAQs

Who seeks out accent coaching?

Accent coaching is a great fit if English is your second language and you're already proficient, but you feel your accent is holding you back, at work or in everyday life, or that people focus more on how you're saying something than on what you're saying.

What are accent coaching sessions like?

Sessions are one-on-one and meet weekly for 50 minutes. Each week we focus on a single target, and you'll get plenty of practice with specific feedback from me. You'll leave with home practice materials to work on until we meet again.

Will I erase my accent?

No, and that is not the goal. You'll still sound like you. Based on your specific goals, we'll work together to modify the aspects of your speech that get in the way of effective communication, so you can focus on what you're saying instead of worrying about being understood. When you begin to feel confident in your communication in English, you can more effectively let your own rich cultural background and knowledge shine through, with your colleagues and with your community.

How much does it cost?

The first consultation call is free. After that, there's a one-hour analysis session and a choice of session packages. See the full details on the pricing section of the Accent Coaching page.

Do we meet online or in person?

We meet online using Google Meet.

Do I get a free consultation call?

Yes. Whether you're just curious or ready to begin, a free 15-minute consultation call is the place to start. If you're curious about accent coaching and want to see whether it's a good fit, we'll talk it through. If you're ready to get started, we'll use the call to discuss how to reach your communication goals.
