FAQs

Do I get a free consultation call?

Yes — every new client gets a free 15-minute consultation call.

Do we meet online or in person?

We meet online using Google Meet.

What are accent coaching sessions like?

Sessions are one-on-one and meet weekly for 50 minutes. Each week we focus on a single target, and you'll get plenty of practice with specific feedback from me. You'll leave with home practice materials to work on until we meet again.

Is homework really necessary? I'm a busy person.

Yes. If you want to change the way you speak, it will take practice, both with me and on your own. With practice at home (a little each day goes a long way), you can make lasting changes to your speech.

Who seeks out accent coaching?

Accent coaching is a great fit if English is your second language, you're already proficient, but you feel your accent gets in the way at work or in everyday life.
