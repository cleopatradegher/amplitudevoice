FAQs

Do we meet online or in person?

Accent coaching sessions are one-on-one and held virtually.

How long is a session, and what does it cost?

Accent coaching sessions are typically 50 minutes long. Contact me for pricing.

Who receives accent coaching?

Accent coaching is a great fit if English is your second language, you're already proficient, but you feel your accent gets in the way at work or in everyday life.

Do I get a free consultation call?

Yes — every new client gets a free 15-minute consultation call.
